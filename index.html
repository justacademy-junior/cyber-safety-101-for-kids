Five rules Byte never forgets

Short rules are easy to remember. Say them out loud at breakfast and they become habits.

⏸️

Pause

Think before you click on pop ups, free prizes and strange links.

🔒

Protect

Keep your name, address, school and passwords private. Ask a parent first.

💬

Polite

Use kind words. A message you type can hurt just like one you say.

👫

People you know

Chat only with people you have met in real life. Strangers stay strangers.

🙋

Parent power

Tell a parent or teacher the moment something feels strange. You will never be in trouble for telling.

Password lab

Type a practice password and watch Byte's strength meter. Use a pretend one only, and never type a real password on any website.
